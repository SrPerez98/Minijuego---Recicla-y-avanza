### Version 2.0 – Stage 4: Game Logic Programming (Initial Prototype)

#### What I did
- Designed the game's visual identity for the main title screen, with the Play, Settings, and Exit buttons integrated into the cover.
- Designed the main menu interface: stylized rusted-metal buttons with toxic slime, each with normal, hover, and pressed states.
- Designed the pause menu (Resume, Settings, Exit) following the same visual style.
- Designed the full HUD: health bar, cleaning progress bar, boss health bar, score counter, level counter, timer, and inventory slots, plus Win and Game Over panels, character portraits, and icons.
- Designed the upgrade selection screen shown after completing a recycling stage (Magnet, More good waste, Greater collection range).
- Created the dialogue system prototype with the scientist professor villain, whose lines carry a double meaning and word self-corrections to hide his intentions. Six dialogue situations: welcome, objective explanation, positive feedback, failure feedback, questions, and farewell.
- Updated the AI usage declaration (Claude, Gemini, and Copilot), documenting the original prompts, their English versions, the results, and the manual modifications for each asset.

#### In progress
- Final difficulty balance adjustments and playtesting together with the QA team.
- Consistency review between art, audio, and mechanics before the final delivery.
